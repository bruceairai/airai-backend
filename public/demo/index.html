AIR AI Demo Messaging Program

This page provides publicly accessible information for A2P 10DLC verification of the AIR AI demo line.

Program Description

AIR AI sends conversational customer care messages to users who contact AIR AI directly. Messages are sent only after the user initiates contact. AIR AI does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How Users Opt In

Users opt in to receive messages by initiating a text message to AIR AI or by calling AIR AI and requesting a text follow-up. AIR AI only sends messages in response to user-initiated contact or an explicit request for follow-up.

  • Inbound SMS: If a user texts AIR AI, AIR AI replies conversationally to collect the user’s request.
  • Phone initiated: If a user calls AIR AI and requests a text follow-up, AIR AI may send a confirmation or response by SMS.

Consent is voluntary. Users are not required to consent to SMS messaging to contact AIR AI by phone.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user’s interaction and request.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 9, 2026

AIR AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information: phone numbers provided when you call or text AIR AI.
  • Message content: the content of SMS messages you send to AIR AI and AIR AI’s responses.
  • Call and messaging metadata: timestamps, delivery status, and related technical data needed to operate the service.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to user-initiated inquiries and provide the requested communications.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the AIR AI service (for example, troubleshooting and quality monitoring).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Mobile Information Sharing

We do not sell or share mobile information (including phone numbers and message content) with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain message and related data only as necessary to provide the service, maintain records, and comply with legal or operational requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@airai.dev.

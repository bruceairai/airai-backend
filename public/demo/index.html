AIR AI Demo Messaging Program

AIR AI sends conversational SMS responses to users who contact AIR AI directly. Messages are sent only after a user initiates contact. AIR AI does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How Users Opt In

Users opt in by initiating a phone call or text message directly to AIR AI. If a user texts AIR AI, AIR AI replies conversationally. If a user calls AIR AI and requests follow-up, AIR AI may send a confirmation SMS.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user interaction.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
